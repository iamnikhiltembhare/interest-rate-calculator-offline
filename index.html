Flat vs Reducing (Diminishing) Balance — What's the Difference?

Reducing balance rate is calculated on the principal outstanding at the end of each period. As you repay principal through EMIs, interest for the next period is charged only on what's left — so the interest portion of your EMI shrinks over time.

Flat rate is calculated on the original principal for the entire tenure, ignoring the principal you've already repaid. This is why a flat rate looks deceptively low compared to a reducing rate that produces the same EMI — a flat rate of 12% typically works out close to double that as an equivalent reducing rate.
